## Rules

- **Format:** JPG, landscape **16:9**, about **1600 × 900 px**, under **300 KB** each (use squoosh.app to compress).
- **Copyright:** only use your **own photos** or images with a free licence (public domain, CC0, CC BY / CC BY-SA) from e.g. **Wikimedia Commons**.
- **Do NOT use:** Premier League or club logos/crests, kit sponsor logos, press/agency photos (Getty, PA, Reuters), film stills, the book cover, or photos of real players/actors unless the licence clearly allows it.
- **Students:** no photos of identifiable students without consent under school policy.
